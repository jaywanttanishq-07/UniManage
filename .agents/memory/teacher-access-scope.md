---
name: Teacher workspace access scope
description: The intended access boundary between faculty tools and student-facing pages.
---

Only Teacher/Faculty accounts may open the teacher workspace. Admin accounts use the separate administration area; student-facing pages, including Notices, remain public and read-only. Teacher Notices is a separate managed board.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose teacher/faculty-only access and requested a distinct teacher notices copy.

**How to apply:** Preserve this separation when changing routes, sidebar links, and role permissions. Do not expose the teacher workspace to Admin or expose student notice editing controls.
